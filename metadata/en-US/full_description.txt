<p dir="auto">
    <strong>AozoraBooks</strong>
     is a modern, cross-platform reading app built with 
    <strong>Kotlin Multiplatform</strong>
    , designed for reading novels in the 
    <a href="https://www.aozora.gr.jp/" rel="nofollow">Aozora Bunko (青空文庫)</a>
     format.
    It supports advanced layout features like ruby annotations and vertical text, and runs on Android, iOS.
</p>
